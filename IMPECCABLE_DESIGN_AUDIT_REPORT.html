"The Generic AI Look" — Structural Anti-Pattern Hall of Fame

Impeccable's design methodology specifically identifies a signature cluster of UI tropes popularized by LLM coding generators. These decorative crutches signal generic automated assembly rather than purposeful, bespoke civic design. The Jakarta CSIRT codebase has contracted 6 major structural anti-patterns:

1. Side-Tab Accent Borders

Thick 3px–8px colored borders plastered on the left or top edges of cards and alerts. The most pervasive AI signature.

Found in 17 project files
2. Repeating Gradient Grid Stripes

Faux 80px hairline vertical stripes rendered in ::before pseudo-elements to fake technical depth without content.

Found in 19 blade views
3. Banned Kicker/Eyebrow Headings

10.5px faint uppercase labels with a 20px horizontal line above headings. Banned by Impeccable's craft floor.

Found in 12 blade views
4. Cookie-Cutter Service Cards

4 identical columns of icon + title + excerpt used as the entire page scaffold. Cards as a lazy substitute for architecture.
